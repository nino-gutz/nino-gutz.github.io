Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Application Purpose

Rclone Google Drive Sync is a personal application used to synchronize files between the owner's Google Drive and Nextcloud accounts.

Data Access and Use

The application accesses Google Drive files only to perform the owner's requested synchronization. It does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for advertising.

Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's private rclone configuration on their server. Synchronization is performed by the owner's self-hosted infrastructure.

Data Sharing

No personal information is collected through this website. File synchronization is limited to the owner's configured accounts.
